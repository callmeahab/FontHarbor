# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content directed at others, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer or hosting-platform channel. Reports will be handled discreetly where possible.

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project community spaces.
